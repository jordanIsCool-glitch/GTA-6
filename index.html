Vice City Nights · Leonida · Fan-made tribute · Out Nov 19, 2026 on PS5 & Xbox Series X|S

Present-day Leonida — fictional Florida, centred on Vice City. Lucia just walked out of Leonida Penitentiary. Jason is the wheelman. One easy score goes wrong, and a conspiracy pulls them across all six regions. This is an original fan game — no Rockstar art, music, logos or dialogue.

Protagonist 1 — Wheelman

🚗 Jason Duval

Ex-soldier turned wheelman in the Leonida Keys, working for Brian Heder. Calm under fire, fastest top speed, lives for the getaway.

Ability (F): Nitro Boost — 2s burst of speed
Car: cyan · Top speed: highest · On foot: steady
Protagonist 2 — First female GTA lead

🌺 Lucia Caminos

Just out of Leonida Penitentiary and done taking orders. Quicker off the line, faster on foot, and she knows how to vanish when the Heat is on.

Ability (F): Ghost — drops Heat fast, cops lose you
Car: pink · Acceleration: highest · On foot: fastest

Six regions: Vice City · Leonida Keys (south, over the bridges) · Grassrivers (west wetlands) · Port Gellhorn · Ambrosia · Mount Kalaga National Park · plus Ocean Drive beach in the east.
